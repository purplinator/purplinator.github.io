# Saxon Stockton
**Astrophysics PhD Student | Texas Christian University**

I am an astophyiscs researcher specializing in spectral line analysis and extracting information from large data sets. My current research focuses is on processing integral field spectroscopy from LVM around the 30 Doradus region of the Large Magellanic Cloud. I am also an enthusiastic karaoke singer, gamer, and traveler.

I am particularly proficient and interested in providing in-depth investigations into data structures.
