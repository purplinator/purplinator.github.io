Caltech SURF and WAVE are a little different than the traditional REU programs. SURF has its own opportunity listings (similar to job openings) and each one has its own timeline. The posting will often include information about the application deadline, eligibility, the project in question, and other relevant details. WAVE is a different program and I'm not as confident about its structure and timeline. I think WAVE is more flexible and may not follow the same rigid application cycle as SURF.
