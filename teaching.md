## Teaching Philosophy
I am a strong believer that any student can learn from the right teacher. I have had many, many conversations with students whose biggest struggle often comes down to a disconnect between the teacher (or professor) and the student. The relationship between students and teachers often times reminds me of Plato's _The Cave_. The teacher is trying to introduce the student to the teacher's world, but the student is viewing the teacher's world through the lens of the student's own understanding of the world. Each step is a breach into the student's understanding of the world, and the wrong guide may struggle to help them. Additionally, my experience in teaching has taught me that the teacher is also only capable of interpreting the world from their own perspective, and the student is also attempting to introduce the teacher into the student's view of the world. Perhaps that is just an analogy for communication in general, but it resonates with me as an apt way to describe the difficulty in being both the teacher and the student. Both are captives to their own perspectives.

We cannot meet the needs of every student, and we cannot force every student to put in effort, but we can make a difference.
